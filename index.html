Meri Pyari didu

Di, pata nahi mujhse kya galti ho gayi ya meri kaunsi baat aapko itni hurt kar gayi, par sach mein mera intention kabhi bhi aapko hurt karna nahi tha. 🥺 Aap mere liye sirf meri badi behen jaisi nahi ho, balki meri woh person ho jiske saath main bina soche kuch bhi share kar sakti hoon, jisse daant bhi kha sakti hoon aur phir bhi apnapan feel karti hoon. Aapka mujhse naraz hona honestly mujhe bahut bura lag raha hai. Ho sakta hai main har baar sahi na hoon, kabhi meri baaton se galti ho jaati hai, par aapko hurt karna kabhi nahi chahti. Agar meri kisi baat se aapko bura laga hai toh dil se sorry, Di. ❤️ Please meri galti ko maaf kar do aur mujhse pehle jaise baat kar lo na. Aapki narazgi se zyada mujhe kuch nahi chahiye, bas aapka woh pehle wala pyaar aur apnapan chahiye. 🫂 Aap mere liye bahut special ho, aur main bilkul nahi chahti ki ek chhoti si baat humare beech distance bana de. Please maan jao na Di… chhoti behen samajh ke ek baar maaf kar do. ❤️🥺
